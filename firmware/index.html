Stack protection is on by default across every architecture. The binaries that miss it are not random: the same core packages (procd, ubox, ubus, uci, fstools, logd, and nftables) ship without canaries on all three images. The number that predicts coverage is the package, not the CPU. Full method and per-package breakdown in the writeup below.

How much of OpenWrt actually ships with stack canaries?
